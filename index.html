Frequently asked questions

Is Tripzey for both people and goods?

Yes. Tripzey supports passenger pooling and private/cargo hire across vehicle types.

How are prices decided?

For pooling, fares are suggested based on distance and demand. For private/cargo, vehicle owners will provide the offer.

Do I need to pay in the app?

You can pay in cash or digitally where supported. Digital options vary by region.
